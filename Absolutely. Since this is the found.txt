# RR Consultancy Service

## Restaurant Recruitment & Hiring Platform

## 1. Overall Website Overview

### What is RR Consultancy Service?

**RR Consultancy Service** is a specialized online recruitment platform built exclusively for the **restaurant and hospitality industry**.

Its purpose is to connect:

* **EMPLOYERS** (Restaurants, Hotels, Cafes, Canteens, Cloud Kitchens, Catering Businesses)
* **APPLICANTS** (Chefs, Cooks, Kitchen Helpers, Bakery Staff, Service Staff, Housekeeping, Hospitality Professionals)

Instead of searching for employees through WhatsApp, Facebook groups, or newspaper ads, employers can post jobs on your platform, and applicants can apply directly.

The website acts as a bridge between employers and applicants while the **ADMIN** manages the entire platform.
